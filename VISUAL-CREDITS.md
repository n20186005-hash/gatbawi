# Visual credits

`public/images/gatbawi-wikimedia-real.webp` is a resized/converted WebP of [Gyeongsan Gatbawi Buddha](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gyeongsan_Gatbawi_Buddha.jpg), by [Barnkim](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Barnkim), dated 18 November 2007. The creator released the photograph into the public domain worldwide, with an unrestricted-use fallback where that dedication is not legally possible. Source identity was checked by comparison of the existing 960×720 WebP and the exact Commons 960×720 preview. This is a historical photograph, not a current conditions report. Attribution and conversion notice are visible beside the hero, documentary figures and footer.

`public/images/gatbawi-logo.webp`, `gatbawi-ridge-illustration.webp`, and `gatbawi-route-motif.webp` are existing project brand/editorial illustrations. They are not documentary photographs, terrain surveys or route maps. Existing `public/icons/` PNG emblems and root SVG favicons are retained project identity graphics.

The two former Heritage Portal/Korea.net gallery photographs were removed because the available source references did not establish per-file reuse permission. No licence is asserted for those retired photographs.
