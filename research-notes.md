# Gatbawi research notes

## Verified baseline

| Topic | Source-backed information for copy | Site use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name and setting | Gatbawi is the informal name associated with the seated stone Buddha on Gwanbong Peak in Palgongsan; the name points to the wide, traditional-hat-like stone above its head. | Intro and history |
| Cultural setting | Korea.net describes the figure as a Unified Silla-era stone Buddha on Gwanbong Peak (853 m) and notes a strong, longstanding visitor tradition of offering sincere prayers. | History and respectful-visit note |
| Two approaches | Korea.net identifies a route through Seonbonsa in Gyeongsan and a Daegu-side route associated with Gwanamsa, described as 1,365 steps. | Route overview |
| Wider park | Palgongsan National Park spans Daegu and Gyeongsangbuk-do; the source describes its natural scenery, ecosystem and cultural resources. | Nearby context |
| Local transit / parking | Daegu Tourism's Gatbawi listing appears in official search results and records parking availability. Korea.net confirms city buses can access key parts of the park and mentions weekend Palgong Bus 3. Exact route numbers, timings, access conditions and fees must be checked before travel. | Practical information |
| Airport | Daegu International Airport's official guide says a public bus stop is about 100 m from the airport. | Airport transfer guidance |
